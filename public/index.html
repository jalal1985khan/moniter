Auto (the default) picks, before each round, the method with the best win rate over your last 200 captured rounds, so you don't have to choose; its own results are scored here like any other method. Nothing is ever sent to the game: no clicks, no real bets. Each round the method picks from earlier rounds, the pretend stake is settled at the table's Back odds against the real result, and it stops by itself at your stop-loss, target or round limit. A round after a capture gap, or when the table's odds haven't been read, is void. The starting balance and limits are filled in from your game balance and your session settings; change them as you like.

Each prediction is made before the round, from earlier rounds only. A win rate inside the "Normal luck range" means the method did no better than chance. With few rounds that range is wide: one method will always look like a winner for a while, and it changes as rounds come in.

Model lab: trained prediction models

ModelNext roundWin ratevs 32-card mathLog score₹100 on its pickValue bets

Every model is trained walk-forward: it predicts each round from earlier rounds only, then learns the result. "vs 32-card math" is its win rate minus the exact model's on the same rounds; it only counts as a real edge if it stays clearly above zero (z above 2.5) over many rounds. Log score measures how good its percentages are (lower is better). Value bets: it bets only when it thinks the table's odds pay more than the risk.
